Brand identity · v1.0 · November 2026

InvoiceFlow

The sheet's corner becomes the flow. A Folded Ledger symbol, a Plus Jakarta Sans Bold wordmark and a navy and green palette built for invoices that get paid.

01 · Starting point

Your file

The uploaded mark already had the right idea: a sheet whose corner is replaced by a quarter circle and a green paid corner. Three problems held it back. The symbol carried no invoice cue, so it read as a generic signal or radar corner. The wordmark cap height was 94% of the symbol height, which made the lockup top-heavy. The F and l were drawn as plain rectangles that did not match the rounded symbol.

The refined system keeps that equity, adds two line items to the sheet, moves the wordmark to a 2.3:1 symbol-to-cap ratio and rebuilds every letter from one typeface.

04 · Construction

Geometry

  • 256-unit square, 28-unit corner radius (11%).
  • Fold: circle of radius 128 (half the sheet) centred on the top-right corner.
  • Paid corner: concentric circle of radius 104, leaving a 24-unit channel. At 24 px the channel is 2.25 px, so it stays open.
  • Line items: 28 units tall with full round ends, 12-unit gap, lengths 116 and 80. Both start at x = 46.
  • Gradients are optional and subtle: green #8BD6B0 to #55C99A across the corner, navy #1C3760 to #142746 across the sheet. Use the -flat files below 48 px and in print.

05 · Legibility

Size tests

Rendered at real pixel size.

One change to your brief: Fresh Green measures 1.95:1 on Off-white, so it fails as text on light surfaces. Ledger Green (#237A57) is the same hue darkened to 5.0:1 and handles links, "Paid" status text and positive amounts on light UI. Keep Fresh Green for the logo and for dark mode. Recommended ratio in layouts: 70% neutrals, 20% navy, 10% green.

The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans Bold converted to outlines, cap height 112 on the 256 grid, tracking −1.2%. Plus Jakarta Sans has geometric bowls with slightly squared terminals, which match the symbol's round-cornered square. It is SIL Open Font License, free for logo and commercial use. Outfit was rejected as too soft; Manrope as too neutral. Never retype the wordmark from a live font: always use the SVG files.

08 · Usage

Rules

  • Clear space: keep x = 1/4 of the symbol height (64 units on the 256 grid) free on all sides.
  • Minimum size: horizontal lockup 96 px wide (screen) or 25 mm (print). Symbol 20 px, below that use the small cut.
  • Backgrounds: the colour logo on Off-white, white or light greys. The dark logo on Midnight or Deep Navy. On photos use mono white over a dark area.
